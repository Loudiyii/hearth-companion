AI Tinkerers · Agents, Everywhere · Paris build day

Hearth Companion

AI agents are about to start spending money for people. We built one for the person who most needs a guardrail — an older adult living alone — and the guardrail is a relative with a tap-to-approve on Telegram. The agent acts freely within limits; above them, it stops and asks.

Bounded agency is the idea. Elder care is the case. One spoken sentence in the room → the agent acts on its own → a relative approves, once, on their phone. The vulnerable person is never the sole approver of a large spend.

01 · How the pieces talk

Two rooms, one backend

Laptop 1 is the elder's room. A phone and laptop 2 are the family. One Next.js app on Vercel is the backend and the dashboard. Everything that must survive a restart or a double-tap lives in Supabase, and the database is also the event bus.

07 · Designing for Marie

What a real impaired user actually does

Taken from Benjamin Code's week wiring his mother's house (early-onset Alzheimer's). He built the eyes and ears; these are the constraints they impose on the hands.

She unplugs anything that blinks at night.No LEDs, no glowing screen at rest. The companion is invisible until it speaks.
She forgot how to use the coffee maker and the TV remote.Zero-action interface. No wake word to remember, no button. She talks; it listens. When something looks wrong, it speaks first.
The same questions, all day, in a loop."Is this my home? Who's your father?" The agent answers the fortieth repetition as warmly as the first. That is a feature, and a demo moment.
Feeling stays, memory goes.Success is not "did she remember" but "does she feel accompanied". Check-ins are reassurance, never surveillance.
She can't approve her own purchases.She can't even answer the phone. So a relative as approver isn't a nice-to-have — it's the only way an agent can ever spend on her behalf. This is why bounded agency is the thesis.
The fall at 7 am, alone in the living room.He happened to be there. Flow 3 is for when nobody is.

"What if she's tricked into approving a scam purchase?"Anything over budget goes to the relative, not to her. The vulnerable person is never the sole approver of a large spend.
"Camera privacy?"One frame every few seconds, analysed for one event, nothing recorded or stored. What the family sees follows the elder's sharing preferences.
"Is this a medical device?"No — assistive awareness that escalates to a human. It never diagnoses and never calls emergency services on its own.
