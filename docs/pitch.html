Hearth Companion

An agent in the room that acts within limits, asks above them, and asks her before it alarms anyone.

AI agents are about to start acting — and spending — for people. So we built one for the person who needs a guardrail most: an older adult living alone, who can't use a phone.

Hearth lives in her room — a laptop's camera, mic and speaker. She talks; it sees her, runs her errands, and pays — but only within a weekly limit her family set. Above it, it stops: her daughter gets a tap-to-approve on Telegram. One tap, one charge, never twice.

And if she falls, the camera doesn't panic anyone. Hearth asks her first: "Marie, are you okay?" Only silence, or a call for help, reaches her daughter — with a photo, in seconds.

Three things it does

Money, with a leash"Order my groceries for the week." Basket €84 > limit €60 → Claire approves on Telegram → one Stripe charge → Hearth tells Marie when it arrives.
It sees her"Do I have my glasses on?" Answers from the live camera frame. Only talks about the room when asked.
Falls: ask firstTwo confirmations (~4 s) → heads-up to Claire → Hearth asks her → "I'm fine" closes it; silence or "help" → photo alert + "I'm on it".

Rules in code, not in the prompt

  • The vulnerable person is never the sole approver of a large spend.
  • One approval, one charge — consumed in a single atomic database statement; a second tap charges nothing.
  • The camera never alarms directly; emergency services are never called automatically — a human decides.
Act within limits · Ask above them · Bring a human in only when it matters.
